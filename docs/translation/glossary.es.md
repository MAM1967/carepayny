# Glosario en español (Spanish glossary)

Pairs with `glossary.md`: same terms, same order, one approved Spanish translation each. Use these terms exactly and consistently in every Spanish page and in `src/i18n/ui/es.json`.

## Never translate (keep in English)

CarePayNY · PPL · PPL@Home · CDPAP · Time4Care · I-9 · W-4 · IT-2104-I · EVV · MLTC · CIN · 401(a) · 401(k) · SSL · Atticus Administration · Calderon v. Public Partnerships, LLC

Also kept as is: 1199SEIU, pplfirst.com, "$" amounts, "1.5×", and the names of organizations, cases and apps (The Legal Aid Society, NY State of Health, Katz Banks Kumin, amNewYork Law).

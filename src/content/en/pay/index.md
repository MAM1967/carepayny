## What pay rules cover home care workers in New York?

On January 1, 2027, the minimum wage for home care aides in New York goes up to {{2027.min.nyc}} an hour in New York City, Long Island and Westchester, and {{2027.min.rest}} an hour in the rest of the state. A **home care aide** is a worker whose main job is helping a person at home with daily activities, such as bathing, dressing or eating.

Four sets of rules decide most home care pay:

- **The home care aide minimum wage.** It is the lowest hourly pay the law allows. It covers agency aides, CDPAP caregivers and most workers hired by a family.
- **Wage parity.** In New York City, Nassau, Suffolk and Westchester, this law adds money or benefits on top of the minimum for home care that Medicaid pays for.
- **Overtime.** Hours over a weekly limit are paid at a higher rate.
- **The 13-hour rule.** It sets when a 24-hour shift can be paid as 13 hours.

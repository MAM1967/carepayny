## How much is wage parity for home care aides in 2027?

From January 1, 2027, wage parity requires at least {{2027.wp.nyc.total}} an hour in total pay and benefits for Medicaid home care in New York City, and {{2027.wp.liw.total}} an hour in Nassau, Suffolk and Westchester. That total is the home care aide minimum wage plus a **benefit portion** of {{2027.wp.nyc.benefit}} an hour in New York City and {{2027.wp.liw.benefit}} an hour in Nassau, Suffolk and Westchester. **Wage parity** is a New York law, the Wage Parity Law. It sets a higher total pay for home care workers downstate.

| Area | Benefit portion | Total per hour, 2026 | Total per hour, 2027 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| New York City | {{2027.wp.nyc.benefit}} | {{2026.wp.nyc.total}} | {{2027.wp.nyc.total}} |
| Nassau, Suffolk or Westchester | {{2027.wp.liw.benefit}} | {{2026.wp.liw.total}} | {{2027.wp.liw.total}} |

The benefit portion stays the same in 2027. The total goes up because the minimum wage goes up on January 1, 2027. More: [New York home care minimum wage for 2027](/pay/minimum-wage-2027/).

## Who is covered by wage parity?

Wage parity covers home care aides when **Medicaid** pays for the care, in whole or in part. Medicaid is government health coverage. The care must be in one of these areas:

- New York City (all 5 boroughs)
- Nassau County
- Suffolk County
- Westchester County

Wage parity does not apply in the rest of New York State. It also does not apply to care that a family pays for privately, without Medicaid.

## Does wage parity cover CDPAP?

Yes. CDPAP is a Medicaid program, so wage parity covers CDPAP caregivers in New York City, Nassau, Suffolk and Westchester. PPL says its rates take wage parity into account. Our page [CDPAP pay rates](/cdpap/pay-rate/) shows how.

## Can wage parity be paid as cash or benefits?

Yes. The employer can pay the benefit portion as cash, as benefits, or as a mix of both. Under the law, benefits that count include:

- health insurance
- pension or retirement money
- education benefits
- paid time off
- wage differentials (extra pay for certain shifts or work)
- extra pay in place of benefits

One limit: a raise that only brings pay up to a new minimum wage does not count toward the benefit portion.

## How does a worker see how wage parity is paid?

The Department of Labor lists two records:

- **A pay notice.** Employers give it at hire and whenever the information changes. It shows the benefit portion. The worker signs and dates it.
- **Pay stubs.** Each pay stub lists each benefit and its hourly rate.

## Why do some pages show a different benefit amount?

Some state web pages still show older, higher benefit amounts. The law changed the benefit portion for all periods on or after January 1, 2024, when the base wage went up. The total per hour still went up. CarePayNY uses the amounts in the current law.

## Where do wage parity complaints go?

The New York State Department of Labor takes complaints about wage parity, minimum wage and overtime. Its complaint form is called LS 223. Its phone line for wage parity questions is 888-525-2267.

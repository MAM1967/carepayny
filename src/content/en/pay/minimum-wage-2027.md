## What is the home care minimum wage in New York in 2027?

On January 1, 2027, the minimum wage for home care aides in New York goes up to {{2027.min.nyc}} an hour in New York City, Long Island and Westchester. In the rest of the state, it goes up to {{2027.min.rest}} an hour. The state announced these rates for 2027.

| Where the work is | 2026 | From January 1, 2027 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New York City (Bronx, Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, Staten Island) | {{2026.min.nyc}} an hour | {{2027.min.nyc}} an hour |
| Nassau, Suffolk or Westchester | {{2026.min.liw}} an hour | {{2027.min.liw}} an hour |
| Any other county | {{2026.min.rest}} an hour | {{2027.min.rest}} an hour |

## Who is a home care aide?

A **home care aide** is a worker whose main job is helping a person at home with daily activities, such as bathing, dressing or eating. The law defines the job by the work, not by who the employer is. Home health aides and personal care aides do this work.

## Does the 2027 minimum cover agency aides?

Yes. Home care agencies must pay their home care aides at least the home care aide minimum wage. In New York City, Nassau, Suffolk and Westchester, aides whose care Medicaid pays for may also be owed **wage parity** on top of the minimum. Wage parity is extra pay or benefits for each hour. More: [Wage parity in NYC, Long Island and Westchester](/pay/wage-parity/).

## Does the 2027 minimum cover CDPAP caregivers?

Yes. In CDPAP, a person who needs care picks their own caregiver, and Medicaid pays the caregiver through PPL. CDPAP caregivers are home care aides, so the minimum covers them. PPL also posts its own base pay for CDPAP. Our page [CDPAP pay rates](/cdpap/pay-rate/) compares PPL's rates with the 2027 minimum.

## Does the 2027 minimum cover workers hired by a family?

Yes, in most cases. When a family hires and pays a home care worker directly, the home care aide minimum still applies. The law leaves out a few groups, such as people who work only **on a casual basis**. That means work done only now and then, not as a regular job. For casual work, the general minimum wage applies instead. More: [Hired directly by a family](/pay/hired-by-a-family/).

## Did the general minimum wage go up in 2027?

No. The general minimum wage for most other jobs does not change on January 1, 2027. It stays {{2027.general.nyc}} an hour in New York City, Long Island and Westchester, and {{2027.general.rest}} an hour in the rest of the state. Only the home care aide minimum goes up on that date.

## Could the rates change again in 2027?

The governor has proposed another raise starting March 1, 2027. It needs the state legislature's approval. If it passes, we will update this page and the [pay calculator](/).

## Does overtime pay change with the new minimum?

The overtime rules stay the same. Overtime is paid at {{ot.times}}. When the hourly rate goes up, overtime pay goes up too. More: [Overtime for home care aides](/pay/overtime/).

Free legal help with unpaid wages depends on where a person lives or works. In New York City, The Legal Aid Society helps low-wage workers with unpaid wages. Upstate and in the Hudson Valley, the Worker Justice Center of New York gives free legal help to low-wage workers. Anywhere in the state, the Department of Labor and the Attorney General's Labor Bureau take complaints about unpaid wages.

The groups below describe their own work on their websites. Each one decides who it can help. Many help only people with lower incomes. CarePayNY is not part of any of them.

## Who can help anywhere in New York State?

- **New York State Department of Labor.** It enforces the state's wage laws, including the home care aide minimum wage and wage parity. It takes wage claims. It's free to file a wage claim. Website: [dol.ny.gov](https://dol.ny.gov/unpaidwithheld-wages-and-wage-supplements).
- **New York State Attorney General's Labor Bureau.** It says it protects workers' rights, including wages. It takes complaints about wages, pay or work conditions, online or by phone: 212-416-8700. It says it will not ask about immigration status. Website: [ag.ny.gov](https://ag.ny.gov/file-complaint/employment).
- **LawHelpNY.** A free website that lists legal aid groups by county and topic, including wages and overtime. It is a directory, not a law office. Website: [lawhelpny.org](https://www.lawhelpny.org/issues/workers-rights/wages-hours-and-overtime).

## Where is free legal help in New York City?

- **The Legal Aid Society, Employment Law Unit.** It helps low-wage and unemployed workers in all five boroughs. It says most of its cases are about wage violations, such as unpaid wages or overtime. Phone: 212-577-3300. Its employment help line is 888-663-6880. Website: [legalaidnyc.org](https://legalaidnyc.org/get-help/employment/).
- **New York Legal Assistance Group (NYLAG), Employment Law Project.** It gives free help to low-wage workers, including recovering unpaid wages. Website: [nylag.org](https://nylag.org/workers-rights/).

## Where is help on Long Island?

- **Make the Road New York.** It has worker groups in Nassau and Suffolk. It says its legal team helps workers recover unpaid wages. Phone: 718-418-7690. Website: [maketheroadny.org](https://maketheroadny.org/issue/workplace-justice).
- The Attorney General's Labor Bureau and the Department of Labor (above) take complaints from Long Island workers too.

## Where is free legal help in Westchester and the Hudson Valley?

- **Worker Justice Center of New York.** It gives free legal services to farmworkers and other low-wage workers in New York, including in wage theft cases. It has offices in Kingston and in Hawthorne (Westchester). Phone: 1-800-724-7020, or 845-331-6615 (Kingston). Website: [wjcny.org](https://www.wjcny.org/).

## Where is free legal help in the Capital Region and the North Country?

- **Legal Aid Society of Northeastern New York, Worker Protection Program.** Its flyer describes free help with wage claims, such as not being paid the right hourly wage or overtime. It serves 16 counties, from Albany and Saratoga to St. Lawrence and Clinton. It generally helps people with a household income at or below 200% of the poverty level. Phone: 833-628-0087. Website: [lasnny.org](https://www.lasnny.org/get-help).
- **Worker Justice Center of New York** (see above) also serves upstate counties.

## Where is free legal help in Central and Western New York?

This covers Central New York, the Mohawk Valley, the Southern Tier, the Finger Lakes and Western New York.

- **Worker Justice Center of New York.** It gives free legal services to low-wage workers across upstate New York. Its main office is in Rochester: 585-325-3050. Toll-free: 1-800-724-7020. Website: [wjcny.org](https://www.wjcny.org/).
- **LawHelpNY** (above) lists other legal aid groups by county.

## What about CDPAP and PPL?

The same groups help CDPAP caregivers with wage problems. For questions about PPL pay, timesheets or sign-up, PPL is the first stop. More: [CDPAP: who handles what](/cdpap/help/). For the PPL court settlement, the settlement administrator handles questions. More: [PPL court settlement](/cdpap/ppl-settlement/).

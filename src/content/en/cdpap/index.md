## What is CDPAP?

CDPAP is a New York Medicaid program. The name stands for Consumer Directed Personal Assistance Program.

In CDPAP, a person who needs home care picks their own caregiver. The caregiver can be a friend or a family member. Medicaid pays the caregiver.

- The person who gets care is called the **consumer**.
- The paid caregiver is called the **personal assistant**, or **PA**.

A PA can do the tasks a personal care aide, a home health aide or a nurse would do. The consumer trains the PA.

## Who can be a PA?

Most adults can be a PA, including many family members. A few people cannot:

- The consumer's spouse (husband or wife)
- The consumer's **designated representative** (the person who makes CDPAP decisions for the consumer)
- The parent of a consumer who is under 21

## Who does what in CDPAP?

The consumer directs the care, the health plan approves the hours, and PPL runs payroll.

| Who | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| The consumer (or their designated representative) | Hires, trains, schedules and supervises the PA. Approves the PA's hours. Plans backup care. |
| The health plan or local social services office | Decides if the person qualifies for CDPAP. Decides how many hours of care are approved. |
| PPL (Public Partnerships LLC) | Runs payroll. Pays the PA, takes out taxes and keeps work records. |

Since April 1, 2025, PPL is the only company that can pay CDPAP workers in New York. The state calls PPL the **Statewide Fiscal Intermediary**. Every CDPAP consumer and PA must work with PPL.

## Who qualifies for CDPAP?

The state lists these rules. The consumer must:

- Have New York Medicaid
- Have a stable medical condition
- Need home care, based on a state-approved assessment (a review of their needs)
- Be able to direct their own care, or have a designated representative who can
- Meet the **Minimum Needs Requirements**, if they are 21 or older

The Minimum Needs Requirements started on September 1, 2025. The consumer must need at least some hands-on help with more than two daily activities, like bathing or dressing. A consumer with dementia or Alzheimer's must need at least supervision with more than one daily activity.

## How is CDPAP different from agency home care?

In CDPAP, the consumer chooses the PA. In agency home care, called Personal Care Services (PCS), a licensed home care agency sends the aide. PCS is another Medicaid home care program. Moving from one program to the other takes a new approval from the health plan.

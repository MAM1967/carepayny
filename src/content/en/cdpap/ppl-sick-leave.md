CDPAP personal assistants (PAs) earn paid sick leave from PPL. PPL calls it **Safe and Sick Leave (SSL)**. PAs earn 1 hour of SSL for every 30 hours worked, up to 56 hours a year. What happens to unused SSL is different in NYC, Nassau, Suffolk and Westchester than in the rest of the state.

## How much paid sick leave do CDPAP PAs earn?

PPL's rules, in effect since May 1, 2026 (or a PA's first day, if later):

- PAs earn 1 hour of SSL for every 30 hours worked.
- PAs can earn up to 56 hours in a calendar year (January to December). After 56 hours, no more SSL is earned that year.
- PAs can use up to 56 hours in a calendar year.
- SSL can be used as soon as it is earned.
- SSL is used in 15-minute blocks.

Example: a PA who works 120 hours in a month earns 4 hours of SSL that month (120 ÷ 30 = 4).

## How do PAs ask for SSL in PPL@Home?

These are PPL's steps:

1. Log in to PPL@Home with a mobile number or email address.
2. Go to the **Timesheet** tab.
3. Under Timesheet Summary, click **+ Submit Time Off**.
4. Pick the Service Period.
5. For each day, choose **Safe and Sick Leave** in the "Select Service" list. Then set the start and end time.
6. Click **Submit**.

Requests can cover scheduled work days from the past 14 days up to 90 days ahead.

PAs who have an approved exception to use paper timesheets pick **Safe and Sick Leave** as the service type at the top right of the paper timesheet. SSL goes on its own paper timesheet. It can't be on the same sheet as hours worked.

## What can SSL be used for?

SSL can be used for any reason the New York State paid sick leave law allows. In NYC, Nassau, Suffolk and Westchester, PPL's policy also allows any reason under New York City law. The state law covers:

- The PA's own illness or injury, or a doctor visit, test, treatment or check-up.
- Caring for a family member who is sick or needs medical care.
- **Safe leave:** time off when the PA or a family member is a victim of domestic violence, a family offense, a sexual offense, stalking or human trafficking. For example, to get help from a shelter or services program, see a lawyer or go to court.

New York City's law also covers time off when the PA's workplace, or a child's school or child care, closes because of a public health emergency.

Privacy and fair treatment, under PPL's policy:

- PPL does not ask for details about a PA's health or the reason for the leave.
- PPL keeps what it learns private, unless the law requires it to share.
- PPL says it will not punish or treat a PA unfairly for using SSL.

## How is SSL different downstate and upstate?

| | NYC, Nassau, Suffolk, Westchester | All other counties |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unused SSL at the end of the year | All unused SSL carries into 2027. From 2027 on, up to 56 hours carry over. Hours above 56 are paid into the PA's 401(a) retirement account. | Unused SSL carries over to the next year. |
| After a PA earns 56 hours in a year | The wage parity money that paid for SSL goes into the PA's 401(a) instead. | No more SSL is earned that year. |
| When a PA stops working for PPL | Unused SSL earned on or after May 1, 2026 is paid into the PA's 401(a), at the rate in effect when it was earned. | Unused SSL is lost. It is not paid out. |
| Paid time off (PTO) earned before May 1, 2026 | It still counts as PTO, but PPL's system shows it as SSL. When a PA asks for leave, PPL uses SSL first. | It is treated as SSL. |

If PPL is not told that a PA stopped working, PPL counts the job as ended after 26 weeks with no paycheck, except in very unusual cases.

## Does SSL count toward overtime?

No. SSL hours do not count toward the 40 hours for overtime, even in a week when the PA also works. SSL also can't be used for an hour the PA is already being paid to work in CDPAP.

## Do NYC PAs get unpaid sick time too?

Yes. PAs who regularly work in New York City also get 32 hours of **unpaid** sick time each calendar year. This is on top of SSL.

- PPL gave all NYC PAs 32 hours on February 22, 2026. After that, the 32 hours come every January 1. PAs hired after February 1, 2026 get the hours on their first day.
- Unused hours do not carry over. They end on December 31.
- Unused hours are not paid out when a PA leaves PPL.
- It can be used for any reason New York City's law allows, in 15-minute blocks.
- In PPL@Home, the steps are the same as for SSL, but the service to choose is **Unpaid Sick Time**.

## Do CDPAP PAs get paid prenatal leave?

Yes. Every PA in New York State gets up to 20 hours of paid prenatal leave in a 52-week period. This is separate from SSL.

- The 52 weeks start the first time the PA uses prenatal leave.
- It covers health care during pregnancy, such as check-ups, tests, procedures, monitoring and talks with a provider. It also covers fertility treatment, such as IVF.
- It does not cover care after the baby is born.
- It is used in 15-minute blocks. Unused hours are lost and are not paid out.
- PPL's policy lists the HR line, 1-833-746-8283, for prenatal leave requests. On a paper timesheet, the service type is "Pre-Natal Leave."

## Where can PAs get answers about sick leave?

- **PPL HR line:** 1-833-746-8283. PPL lists this number in all of its sick leave policies.
- **New York State Department of Labor:** enforces the state's paid sick leave law and takes complaints. It's free to file a complaint.
- **NYC Department of Consumer and Worker Protection (DCWP):** enforces New York City's sick leave law. It can be reached through 311 by asking for "Paid Safe and Sick Leave."

## When does PPL pay CDPAP caregivers?

PPL pays CDPAP personal assistants (PAs) once a week, on Thursday. The money comes by direct deposit to a bank account or by paper check. The PA picks the method on PPL's Payment Method Form.

## What are PPL's timesheet deadlines?

A PA's hours for the week are due to PPL by Saturday at 11:59 p.m. The consumer approves them by Sunday at noon. PPL pays them on Thursday of the next week.

| What | Deadline (New York time) |
| --- | --- |
| PA sends in the week's hours | Saturday, 11:59 p.m. |
| Consumer or designated representative approves the hours | Sunday, 12:00 noon |
| Pay for those hours | Thursday of the next week |

PPL does not pay hours that the consumer has not approved. Paper timesheets have the same Saturday deadline.

Example: a PA works Sunday to Saturday. The PA sends in the hours by Saturday night. The consumer approves them by Sunday noon. PPL pays the PA on Thursday.

PPL posts a full list of 2026 pay dates in its "NY CDPAP Pay Schedule 2026."

## How do PAs send in their hours?

There are three ways: the Time4Care app, a phone line, or a paper timesheet.

- **Time4Care app.** The PA clocks in and out on a phone. The app works offline and sends the hours when the phone is back online. PPL says to get the app only from the Apple App Store or Google Play.
- **Phone.** PAs and consumers without internet can clock in, clock out or approve hours by calling 1-833-278-3959.
- **Paper timesheet.** Only with an approved exception from PPL.

Time4Care and the phone line use **EVV** (Electronic Visit Verification). EVV records when and where care happens.

## Does PPL round time?

Yes. PPL rounds each shift. How it rounds depends on the service code:

- **15-minute codes:** up to 7 minutes past a quarter hour rounds down. More than 7 minutes rounds up. Example: 5 hours 19 minutes becomes 5 hours 15 minutes.
- **Hourly codes:** up to 29 minutes past the hour rounds down. More than 29 minutes rounds up. Example: 5 hours 43 minutes becomes 6 hours.

When time rounds up, it uses more of the consumer's approved hours.

## How long does direct deposit take to start?

New or changed bank details for PPL direct deposit can take 1 to 2 pay periods to start working. Until then, PPL mails a paper check to the address on file.

## Does PPL pay for every hour worked?

PPL pays only for approved hours. The health plan or local social services office approves a set number of hours. This is called the **service authorization**. PPL pays only for hours inside it. The approved hours reset every Sunday at 12:00 a.m.

The consumer can see the approved hours, and how many are left, in PPL@Home.

## What does "Pended" mean in PPL@Home?

"Pended" means PPL is holding the payment. PPL@Home shows the status of each timesheet. Clicking it shows the reason. Our page [I wasn't paid: common reasons](/cdpap/not-paid/) explains each one.

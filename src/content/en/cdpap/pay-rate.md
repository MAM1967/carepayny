## Two numbers: the law and PPL's rate

| Where the consumer lives | State minimum (the law) | PPL's base pay for CDPAP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New York City (Bronx, Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, Staten Island) | {{min.nyc}} an hour | {{ppl.nyc}} an hour |
| Nassau, Suffolk or Westchester | {{min.liw}} an hour | {{ppl.liw}} an hour |
| Any other county | {{min.rest}} an hour | {{ppl.rest}} an hour |

Both numbers start {{ppl.effective}}.

- The **state minimum** is the lowest pay the law allows for home care aides. The New York State Department of Labor sets it. CDPAP caregivers are covered by it.
- **PPL's base pay** is the rate PPL says it pays CDPAP caregivers. In New York City, Nassau, Suffolk and Westchester, it is higher than the state minimum.

PPL's rate depends on where the **consumer** lives, not where the PA lives. PPL says its rates are set, not negotiated. The PA's offer letter lists their base rate.

## Wage parity (NYC, Long Island and Westchester)

In New York City, Nassau, Suffolk and Westchester, a state law called the **Wage Parity Law** adds to the minimum. It covers Medicaid-paid home care, including CDPAP.

| Area | Minimum wage | Extra wage parity amount | Total per hour |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| New York City | {{min.nyc}} | {{wp.nyc.benefit}} | {{wp.nyc.total}} |
| Nassau, Suffolk or Westchester | {{min.liw}} | {{wp.liw.benefit}} | {{wp.liw.total}} |

The employer can pay the extra amount as cash, as benefits, or as a mix of both. PPL says its rates take wage parity into account. PPL pays part of it as cash (its base pay is above the minimum) and part as benefits. Examples from PPL's policies:

- **Paid sick leave:** part of each hour's wage parity amount goes toward paid sick leave.
- **Retirement:** PAs in these areas who are not in a PPL health plan get wage parity money put into a **401(a)** retirement account.

More on benefits: [Overtime, holidays, sick leave and benefits](/cdpap/overtime-holidays-sick-leave/).

Wage parity does not apply in the rest of the state.

## Overtime

Hours over {{ot.hours}} in a week are paid at {{ot.times}}. PPL has extra rules on overtime and holiday pay. They are on [Overtime, holidays, sick leave and benefits](/cdpap/overtime-holidays-sick-leave/).

## Live-in days

For live-in (per-diem) service codes, PPL pays a flat {{livein.hours}} hours per day. Only one PA per day gets that payment.

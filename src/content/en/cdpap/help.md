Each office handles a different part of CDPAP. This page lists what each one does.

## PPL (payroll, sign-up, timesheets)

PPL runs CDPAP payroll for the whole state.

| For | Phone or contact |
| --- | --- |
| Pay, timesheets and general questions | 1-833-247-5346 |
| Email | nycdpap@pplfirst.com |
| Clock in or out, or approve hours, by phone | 1-833-278-3959 |
| HR, health checks and medical exemptions | 1-833-746-8283 |
| Benefits (GSA National) | 1-888-250-8303 |
| Wisely Pay card | 1-866-313-6901 |

PPL has offices that see people by appointment.

## CDPAP facilitators

Facilitators are community groups that work with PPL. They help with sign-up, forms and the EVV time system. Some speak languages other than English. PPL lists the approved facilitators, with the counties and languages each one serves, on its website. PPL asks people to call PPL before sharing information with anyone who says they are a facilitator but is not on the list.

## The health plan or local social services office (approved hours)

The consumer's health plan (often a Managed Long Term Care plan) or local social services office decides:

- If the consumer qualifies for CDPAP
- How many hours of care are approved

Their phone number is on the consumer's member card and notices. When a plan cuts or denies hours, it must send a written notice that explains the decision and how to appeal.

## New York State Medicaid

- Medicaid Helpline: 1-800-541-2831. It helps with Medicaid questions, such as finding a Medicaid ID number (CIN).
- Fair hearings (an appeal to the state about home care decisions): 1-800-342-3334

## New York State Department of Labor (wages)

The [New York State Department of Labor](https://dol.ny.gov/) enforces the state's wage laws, including the home care aide minimum wage. It takes wage claims. It's free to file a wage claim.

## The Legal Aid Society (free legal help in NYC)

The Legal Aid Society gives free legal help to New Yorkers who qualify, including with Medicaid home care and unpaid wages. Phone: 212-577-3300.

## The PPL settlement administrator

Atticus Administration handles the $162 million PPL settlement for downstate PAs: 1-800-314-2601, or PublicPartnershipsSettlement@AtticusAdmin.com. More on the [PPL court settlement](/cdpap/ppl-settlement/) page.

## NY State of Health (health insurance)

The state's health insurance marketplace: 1-855-355-5777.

Newest first. Each entry links to its source at the bottom of this page.

| Date | What changed |
| --- | --- |
| November 10, 2026 | A federal judge gave final approval to the $162 million PPL settlement for downstate PAs. More: [The PPL court settlement](/cdpap/ppl-settlement/). |
| July 22, 2026 | The Department of Health issued MLTC Policy 26.02. PPL will send health plans a report every two weeks on PAs who work very long hours. |
| July 21, 2026 | Settlement notices were sent to downstate PAs by email and text. |
| July 1, 2026 | A federal judge gave early (preliminary) approval to the PPL settlement. |
| May 27, 2026 | Some new PAs began getting their health check from The IMA Group instead of Mobile Health. |
| May 1, 2026 | New paid sick leave policy for downstate PAs. Wage parity money for part-time downstate PAs and downstate PAs without a PPL health plan goes to a 401(a). |
| April 30, 2026 | PPL's MEC (BasicWellness) health plan and Flex Card plan ended. |
| March 31, 2026 | Deadline for PAs who started in 2025 to finish PPL's required training. |
| February 22, 2026 | PAs in New York City started getting 32 hours of unpaid sick time a year. |
| January 1, 2026 | All PAs got a raise of $0.55 an hour. PPL's new base rates started. More: [Your pay rate in CDPAP](/cdpap/pay-rate/). |
| September 1, 2025 | The Minimum Needs Requirements started for new assessments and reassessments of people 21 and older. |
| April 1, 2025 | PPL became the only company that can pay CDPAP workers in New York. |

PPL does not pay a CDPAP personal assistant (PA) until both the PA and the consumer have finished signing up with PPL. Until then, PPL@Home shows the PA's timesheets as "Pended" (payment on hold).

## What you see

PPL@Home shows the timesheet as "Pended." The reason is one of these:

- **Provider is not Paperwork Complete**
- **Provider must have SSN**
- **Provider is not PA for Consumer**
- **Consumer is not enrolled during Time Entry's Date of Service**

## What it means

PPL pays a PA only after both the PA and the consumer have finished signing up.

| Message | The usual reason |
| --- | --- |
| Provider is not Paperwork Complete | One or more of the PA's forms is missing or not accepted. This includes the I-9, tax forms, the offer letter or the health check. |
| Provider must have SSN | PPL has no Social Security number for the PA. |
| Provider is not PA for Consumer | The PA's account is not linked to the consumer's account. |
| Consumer is not enrolled… | The consumer had not finished signing up on that date. The consumer must finish registration, sign the **Memorandum of Understanding (MOU)** and have approved hours. |

## Where the records are

- **PPL@Home** lists each form and whether it is done.
- PAs who missed a required health check are "on hold" and cannot send in time. It takes 2 to 3 business days to come off hold after passing the health check.

## Who handles each part

- **The PA:** their own forms, I-9 documents, Social Security number and health check
- **The consumer or designated representative:** the consumer's sign-up, the MOU, and asking PPL to link the PA
- **PPL:** linking accounts and checking forms. PPL's phone line is 1-833-247-5346.
- **A CDPAP facilitator**, if the consumer uses one: help with forms and sign-up

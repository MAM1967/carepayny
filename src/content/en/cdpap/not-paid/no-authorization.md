## What you see

PPL@Home shows the timesheet as "Pended." The reason is one of these:

- **There is no authorization for the Consumer's Plan they are currently enrolled under**
- **There is no authorization for Time Entry's service code**
- **Time Entry's Date of Service is not within Authorization From/To Date**

## What a service authorization is

A **service authorization** is the approval for the consumer's care. It comes from the consumer's **health plan** (often a Managed Long Term Care plan) or the **local social services office**. It says:

- How many hours of care are approved
- Which service codes can be used
- The start and end dates

The health plan or social services office sends it to PPL. PPL can pay only for hours inside it. PPL does not decide the number of hours.

## Why it can be missing

Some ways this can happen:

- The consumer changed health plans, and the new plan has not sent an authorization yet.
- The old authorization ended, and the new one has not reached PPL.
- The time was entered under a service code that is not on the authorization.

## Where the records are

The **Authorizations** tab in PPL@Home shows what PPL has on file: the hours, codes and dates. PPL says this tab updates every day.

## What PPL says happens next

PPL says that once the authorization is fixed, the time is paid in the next payroll.

## Who handles each part

- **The health plan or local social services office:** creates the authorization and sends it to PPL. Their phone number is on the consumer's member card or notices.
- **The consumer or designated representative:** is the health plan's member, and can ask the plan about it
- **PPL:** loads the authorization and pays the hours. PPL's phone line is 1-833-247-5346.

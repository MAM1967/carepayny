## What you see

The pay is there, but it is for fewer hours than the PA sent in. Or the amount is lower than expected.

## Reasons PPL lists

**1. Hours went past the approved hours.**
PPL pays only for hours inside the consumer's **service authorization**. The approved hours reset every Sunday at 12:00 a.m. Hours past the limit show the message "Time Entry's Units exceed remaining on Authorization."

**2. Rounding.**
PPL rounds each shift. On hourly service codes, up to 29 minutes past the hour rounds down. Example: 5 hours 25 minutes is paid as 5 hours. On 15-minute codes, up to 7 minutes past a quarter hour rounds down.

**3. The consumer was in the hospital.**
PPL does not pay for time when the consumer is in a hospital or other health care facility. The day they go in and the day they come home are the exceptions.

**4. Overlapping time.**
If two time entries cover the same time, PPL holds one. This can happen with two PAs for one consumer, or one PA with two consumers.

**5. Live-in (per-diem) days.**
Live-in days are paid as a flat {{livein.hours}} hours. Only one PA per day gets that payment. PPL denies other timesheets for the same day.

**6. Holiday pay was lower than expected.**
Holiday premium pay and overtime premium pay are not paid together in the same week. Overtime hours lower the holiday premium hour for hour. More on this: [Overtime, holidays, sick leave and benefits](/cdpap/overtime-holidays-sick-leave/).

**7. The pay rate itself.**
PPL lists a base pay rate for each area. The rate is also in the PA's offer letter. More on this: [Your pay rate in CDPAP](/cdpap/pay-rate/).

## Where the records are

- The **Timesheet** tab in PPL@Home shows each entry and its status.
- The **Authorizations** tab shows the approved hours and how many are left.
- The PA's pay stub shows the hours and rate PPL paid.

## Who handles each part

- **The health plan or local social services office:** the number of approved hours
- **The consumer or designated representative:** scheduling inside the approved hours, and approving timesheets
- **PPL:** payroll and pay stubs. PPL's phone line is 1-833-247-5346.

PPL pays a CDPAP personal assistant (PA) on Thursday for the hours sent in by Saturday at 11:59 p.m. and approved by the consumer by Sunday at 12:00 noon. Hours sent in after the Saturday deadline are not in that Thursday's pay. Hours the consumer does not approve are not paid.

## What you see

Thursday comes, and the pay for some or all of last week's hours is not there.

## The deadlines

| What | Deadline (New York time) |
| --- | --- |
| PA sends in the week's hours | Saturday, 11:59 p.m. |
| Consumer or designated representative approves the hours | Sunday, 12:00 noon |
| Pay for those hours | Thursday of the next week |

PPL's rules say:

- Hours sent in after Saturday at 11:59 p.m. are not in that Thursday's pay.
- **Hours the consumer does not approve are not paid.**
- Paper timesheets have the same Saturday deadline.

PPL's "NY CDPAP Pay Schedule 2026" lists the deadline for each pay date.

## Approving hours

The consumer or designated representative approves hours in one of these ways:

- In PPL@Home, on the **Timesheet** tab
- By phone, at 1-833-278-3959. The phone line asks if the hours and days are right. A shift can be approved or rejected.

## Where the records are

The **Timesheet** tab in PPL@Home shows each entry and its status: sent in, approved, paid or "Pended."

## Who handles each part

- **The PA:** sends in hours by the Saturday deadline
- **The consumer or designated representative:** approves hours by Sunday at noon
- **PPL:** runs payroll. PPL's phone line is 1-833-247-5346.

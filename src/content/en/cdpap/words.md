**401(a)**
A retirement account. PPL puts wage parity money into it for some downstate PAs.

**Authorization (service authorization)**
The approval of the consumer's care. It says how many hours are approved, which service codes can be used, and the dates. It comes from the health plan or local social services office. PPL pays only for hours inside it.

**CDPAP**
Consumer Directed Personal Assistance Program. A New York Medicaid program where a person who needs home care picks their own caregiver.

**CIN**
Client Identification Number. The consumer's Medicaid ID number. It is on the Medicaid card.

**Consumer**
The person who gets care in CDPAP.

**Designated representative**
A person who makes CDPAP decisions for a consumer who can't direct their own care. A designated representative cannot also be the consumer's PA.

**EVV (Electronic Visit Verification)**
A system that records when and where care happens. PPL's Time4Care app and phone line use EVV.

**Facilitator (CDPAP facilitator)**
A community group that works with PPL to help consumers and PAs with sign-up and forms.

**Fiscal intermediary**
The company that runs payroll for CDPAP. Since April 1, 2025, PPL is the only one in New York. The state calls it the **Statewide Fiscal Intermediary**.

**Health plan (MLTC)**
The consumer's Medicaid health plan. Many CDPAP consumers are in a Managed Long Term Care (MLTC) plan. The plan approves care hours.

**I-9**
A U.S. government form. It shows who a worker is and that they are allowed to work in the U.S.

**LDSS**
Local Department of Social Services. In some cases, this office approves CDPAP hours instead of a health plan.

**Live-in (per-diem)**
A service code for a PA who stays with the consumer. PPL pays a flat number of hours per day.

**Minimum Needs Requirements**
Rules that started September 1, 2025. They set how much help with daily activities a person 21 or older must need to qualify for CDPAP.

**MOU (Memorandum of Understanding)**
An agreement between the consumer and PPL. The consumer signs it to finish signing up.

**Overtime**
Pay at a higher rate for hours over {{ot.hours}} in a week.

**PA (personal assistant)**
The paid caregiver in CDPAP.

**"Paperwork Complete"**
PPL's term for a PA who has finished every sign-up form. PPL pays only PAs who are Paperwork Complete.

**"Pended"**
A status in PPL@Home. It means PPL is holding a payment. Clicking it shows the reason.

**PPL (Public Partnerships LLC)**
The company that runs CDPAP payroll in New York.

**PPL@Home**
PPL's website for CDPAP accounts, forms, timesheets and approvals.

**SSL (Safe and Sick Leave)**
PPL's name for paid sick leave.

**Time4Care**
PPL's phone app for clocking in and out.

**Wage parity**
A New York law for Medicaid home care in New York City, Nassau, Suffolk and Westchester. It requires a set amount of total pay per hour, as wages, benefits or both.

A PA must finish signing up with PPL before PPL can pay them. PPL calls this being **"Paperwork Complete."**

## Step 1: The consumer comes first

Before a PA can sign up, three things must be true:

1. The consumer is signed up with PPL.
2. The consumer has a valid **service authorization** (approved hours) from their health plan or local social services office.
3. The consumer has asked PPL to link the PA to their account.

## Step 2: Forms in PPL@Home

PPL@Home is PPL's website for CDPAP. The PA creates an account and fills out these forms:

| Form | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Offer Letter | One for each consumer. It lists the PA's base pay rate. |
| Personal Assistant Agreement | The PA's agreement with PPL |
| IRS Form W-4 | Federal tax withholding |
| NY Form IT-2104-I | New York tax withholding |
| USCIS Form I-9, with documents | Shows the PA is allowed to work in the U.S. See [Missing I-9](/cdpap/not-paid/missing-i9/). |
| Payment Method Form | Direct deposit or paper check |
| Health Assessment | A health check (see below) |

PPL gives each person a **PPL ID**. It may come by email or text.

## Step 3: The health check

New PAs have a health check before they start. PPL assigns the provider: Mobile Health or The IMA Group. The PA cannot choose the provider. The To Do List tab in PPL@Home shows which one.

The first health check includes:

- A physical exam
- Proof of immunity to measles, mumps and rubella (MMR)
- A TB test, plus TB risk questions

After that, there is a shorter health check every year. Results usually take 3 to 5 business days.

PAs may use their own doctor, but they pay for it.

A PA who misses a required health check is put "on hold." On hold, the PA cannot send in time. After passing the health check, it takes 2 to 3 business days to come off hold.

## Step 4: Time4Care and training

The PA downloads the Time4Care app and logs in with the PPL@Home username and password.

- **EVV training** (how to clock in and out) must be done by the PA's start date.
- **Other required training** has due dates listed in PPL@Home.
- Training is free. PPL pays for training time at the PA's regular rate.
- PAs who worked with a CDPAP company before PPL still take PPL's training.

## Step 5: The start date

When everything is done, PPL checks the consumer and PA. Then PPL sends the PA a start date.

## What are the ways to sign up with PPL?

PPL lists these options:

- PPL@Home online, at pplfirst.com/cdpap
- PPL's phone line: 1-833-247-5346
- Email: nycdpap@pplfirst.com
- A PPL office, by appointment
- A **CDPAP facilitator**: a community group that works with PPL and helps with sign-up. PPL lists approved facilitators on its website.
